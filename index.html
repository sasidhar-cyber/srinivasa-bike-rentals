CLEAR & SIMPLE

Rental Rules & Policies.

A few things to know before you collect your ride.

01 · Licence & ID

Bring a valid driving licence and government-issued photo ID. Confirm any additional verification requirements with us before pickup.

02 · Daily distance

150 km are included per rental day. Additional distance is charged at ₹4 per kilometre.

03 · Return time

Additional usage time is charged at ₹60 per extra hour. Agree on pickup and return timings with us in advance.

04 · Fuel & helmet

Fuel is not included. A helmet is provided; please confirm availability and any passenger helmet requirements before pickup.

05 · Availability

All rentals are subject to vehicle availability. Your enquiry is not a confirmed booking until we confirm it on WhatsApp.

06 · Payment & delivery

No online payment or online booking is available on this website. Ask us directly about payment method and delivery availability or charges.
